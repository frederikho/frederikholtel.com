I am a PhD candidate and research associate at the Chair of Environmental Economics at RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau, supervised by Prof. Dr. Daniel Heyen. My research is in environmental and climate economics, in particular carbon credit markets, carbon dioxide removal and mitigation deterrence, and cap-and-trade policy. I also work on energy in emerging economies, with a project on solar panel adoption and electricity outages in Pakistan.

Before my PhD, I studied International Economic Policy and Political Science (M.A. double degree) at Sciences Po Paris and Free University Berlin, Economics at the University of Bonn, and International Relations at Rhine-Waal University of Applied Sciences. I was a fellow of the German Academic Scholarship Foundation.

I have also worked in data science and AI, as a researcher at the German Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI) and as an independent IT consultant and web developer. I like communicating research visually, through interactive visualisations and mathematical animations.
